After last week's run of interactive attack demos for the cryptography course, this week was noticeably quieter. Good kind of quiet — focused rather than slow.

Most of my time went into [`agentwerk`](https://github.com/canvascomputing/agentwerk), the Rust crate I've been building at Canvas Computing. It's aimed at giving any application agentic capabilities with minimal friction — the kind of thing where you don't want to bolt on a full framework just to add a bit of LLM-driven decision making. The design keeps nudging me towards simplicity, which is harder than it sounds in the agent space where everything tends to accumulate abstractions.

Nothing else dramatic to report this week. Sometimes a week is just heads-down on one thing, which is fine.
